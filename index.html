01About

I am an M.S. student at the School of Electrical Engineering, Kookmin University. I work on hardware for energy-efficient AI: analog and in-memory computing with RRAM crossbars and SRAM, neuromorphic circuits, and learning rules such as equilibrium propagation that let neural networks train directly on analog hardware. I also work on compact and binary neural networks for edge devices.
